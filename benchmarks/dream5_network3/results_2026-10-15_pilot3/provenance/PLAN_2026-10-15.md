# Approved DREAM5 Network 3 sampling pilot

Protocol declared before Network 3 inference/scoring on 2026-10-15, after user
review and authorization. This is a prospective small comparison on real
E. coli microarray expression, not a new parameter-optimization exercise.

## Question and primary comparison

Does the operational PR70 fixed-size sampling change improve recovery of the
supplied E. coli regulatory reference after standard MI filtering and DPI?

The primary contrast is **80% without replacement versus the retained legacy
with-replacement path**, using one pinned PR70 executable. The primary endpoint
is **post-DPI average precision (AP) at nominal p_b=1e-7** on the fixed labeled
universe. AP is the tie-aware stepwise precision-recall summary defined by this
benchmark, not a claim to the official DREAM5 leaderboard AUPR score.
Reference TP/FP, precision, sensitivity, specificity and AUROC are supporting
readouts; raw-MI rankings and a common-MI-cutoff comparison are controls.

## Five direct native stages per arm/seed

| Stage | Cutoff/pruning flags | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| raw | `-p 1 -t 0 -e 1` | Complete MI ranking, before filtering/DPI |
| nominal_pre | `-p 1e-7 -e 1` | Nominal affine filtering, no DPI |
| nominal_post | `-p 1e-7 -e 0` | Main filtered/DPI network |
| fixed_pre | `-p 1 -t 0.03759353615996272 -e 1` | Common numerical cutoff, no DPI |
| fixed_post | `-p 1 -t 0.03759353615996272 -e 0` | Common cutoff plus standard DPI |

Common numerical MI is the historical Network 1 80% affine threshold. It is
declared here before E. coli scoring, not optimized against E. coli reference
labels or asserted to be a newly calibrated p-value. This controls the cutoff
difference, not the sample-count difference.

Each stage is computed directly from expression with the same engine, input,
arm and seed. Do not replay rounded `.adj` values for the primary DPI result:
serialization can change strict threshold/DPI decisions. Native MI values
shared between stages must agree at their serialized precision; post-DPI
outputs must be subsets of the appropriate pre-DPI networks. Header checks
allow native output rounding rather than re-filtering rounded MI values.

## Reference and metric definitions

Use exactly the **152,280 supplied labeled directed non-self pairs**:
2,066 positives and 150,214 operational negatives. Other output pairs are
unlabeled, reported separately and never counted as false positives.

Raw ranking includes every labeled pair, assigning a genuine computed MI=0
omission to zero only after validated successful complete native output.
For fixed-universe filtered-network AP/AUROC, omitted eligible pairs share a
bottom-score zero tie. AUROC gives equal-MI positive-negative pairs half credit;
AP groups whole equal-MI score levels.

Top-k displays use k=100, 500, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 20,000, 50,000 and 100,000.
TP/FP at a boundary MI tie use the expectation under uniform within-tie order.
For thresholded networks, k above the labeled emitted pool is unavailable;
do not invent additional called edges from the omitted bottom tie. EPR uses
one fixed random precision, `2066 / 152280`, not an arm-specific shuffled pool.

DPI diagnostics report pre/post counts and pruning fractions for the full
inferred network and the labeled subset, plus positive/negative removal and
TP retention. Fractions are computed within each run before summarizing;
they are not ratios of median edge counts. No global MI pooling or consensus.

## Execution and technical gates

1. Verify source/prepared inputs, executable/configuration hashes and available
   memory/disk. Commit the protocol/implementation before the first scores.
2. Smoke: seed 1, legacy and u80, all five stages (**10 native jobs**).
3. Require successful completion, exact source/sample counts, expected flags
   and thresholds, consistent MI values, valid DPI subsets and a complete
   fixed scoring universe. A technical failure stops expansion for inspection.
4. If the smoke is technically valid, finish all three seeds/four arms
   **regardless of metric direction**: 60 native jobs total, reusing the ten
   completed smoke artifacts. Use up to **12 workers** after the smoke timing
   and memory check. Completed jobs are hash-verified; incomplete jobs are
   preserved, not silently overwritten.
5. Independently check scoring and provenance, report all arms/stages and
   paired changes, and publish compact evidence. Large adjacencies/logs stay
   local under ignored `runs/`. A further 20- or 100-seed extension requires
   another scope decision; it is not automatically selected by favorable scores.

## Interpretation limits

This reference is incomplete. A reference-negative pair can be an unrecorded
real interaction; benchmark FP reductions are not proof of eliminating
biologically false edges. Expression association does not establish direction
or causality. The supplied TF list defines candidate source roles.

The 805 profiles span heterogeneous experiments, replicates and time courses;
they are not 805 established independent biological observations. Profile-level
sampling matches Network 1; experiment/block-aware sampling would be a separate
design. Three computational seeds provide an exploratory readout, not reliable
biological uncertainty or statistical significance.

An AP/precision gain accompanied by TP/recall loss is a tradeoff, not an
unqualified improvement. A raw-MI gain without a post-DPI gain supports only a
stage-specific conclusion. Report unfavorable results and do not retune MI or
DPI against this reference to manufacture a positive comparison.
