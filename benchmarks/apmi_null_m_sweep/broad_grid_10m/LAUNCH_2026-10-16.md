drift is a failure, not an automatic retry.

To request a cooperative stop (not issued at launch):

```powershell
python benchmarks/apmi_null_m_sweep/broad_grid_10m/generate.py stop --plan runs/apmi_null_broad_grid_10m_20261016/plan.json
```

That prevents new jobs while active streams finish and checkpoint. Completed
streams can be revalidated on explicit resume. Failed/interrupted attempts
remain preserved and block automatic retries or truncation of existing data.
Do not run a second controller concurrently.

## Statistical seal and what happens next

This controller performs generation and integrity checks only. It does not
fit models, compute CDFs/quantiles/cutoffs, select parameters, evaluate held-out
calibration or change engine defaults. No such statistical artifacts existed
in the new run at launch verification.

Analysis implementation may be completed while generation runs, but must be
tested/hash-frozen before opening any new fitting/development statistics.
All final validation and final-test-m fitting data remain sealed until the
selected procedure's once-only refit, primary function/domain and hashes are
frozen. Completion of all 400 jobs does not automatically open those streams.
Generation completion is not a claim of successful calibration, and rare-p
uncertainty and all failed/unresolved outcomes remain reportable results.
