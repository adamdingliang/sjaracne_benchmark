# Expanded exact-m AP-MI calibration: 10M + 10M

This is a prospective extension requested on October 15, 2026, after reviewing
the completed 5M + 5M calibration. The user requested 10 million fitting and
10 million independent validation draws and additional m values below 1000.
It does not replace or modify the earlier frozen experiment.

## Fixed scientific design

- Exact m grid, sorted: 30, 50, 80, 90, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 400, 500,
  516, 600, 644, 700, 725, 800, 900, 1000. Nineteen sizes in total.
- Retain every previous size, including the m=1000 upper anchor, and add
  m=200, 250, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900.
- Every m gets 10,000,000 fresh fitting draws and 10,000,000 independent
  validation draws: 380 million null pairs and 3,040,000,000 raw bytes total.
  No previous fitting/held-out stream is reused or extended.
- New base seeds: fit=20261017, validation=20261018. Actual fit seed=base+m;
  actual validation seed=base+1000000000+m. These are RNG seeds, not dates.
- Use the same shared AP-MI kernel, Npar=40, independent uniform rank
  permutations and unique ordinal ranks. No expression data or edge-reference
  labels enter the simulation or fitting.
- Pinned generator SHA256:
  `08224b6b20e29628be6d89d22eb7e355f8c6a05623e06f801fae5347a70a7f18`.
- Keep all scientific criteria unchanged: primary GPD q=0.995, at least
  20,000 strict primary exceedances, three valid fits at q=0.9925, 0.995,
  0.9975, relative cutoff range <=10% at p=0.0005. The increased draw count
  naturally supplies more tail observations; the acceptance rule is not relaxed.
- Requested model probability range [0.0005,0.001], default/minimum=0.0005.
  Gate 2 independently checks p=0.001 and 0.0005. Expected validation counts
  are 10,000 and 5,000. Preserve the per-model family-confidence setting .99
  and conservative family size ten: .999 pointwise Clopper-Pearson intervals.
  This is not simultaneous .99 coverage over all nineteen m values.
- Fit data alone determine thresholds and GPD parameters. Held-out data do
  not refit/select models or adjust acceptance criteria. Scientific rejections
  remain rejections; do not rerun until accepted or silently use a fallback.

## Scope and interpretation

More draws reduce Monte Carlo uncertainty; they do not repair a misspecified
GPD. Narrower validation intervals may reveal additional discrepancies. Keep
the earlier labels and legacy `cutoff_p1e-7` reports byte-identical; corrected
new summaries must explicitly label p=0.0005 and p=0.001.

Raw streams stay local under ignored runs/. Publish compact reports, plots,
accepted exact-m models and provenance only. Do not change engine code,
production calibration files or defaults, interpolate between m values, or
claim biological FDR, optimal TF/SIG settings, or accuracy with ties/related
observations. The endpoint checks do not validate every interior probability.
